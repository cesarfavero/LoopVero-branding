# Contributing to LoopVero

LoopVero currently consists of design and documentation. A contribution license and public contribution channel have not yet been established. Do not submit code until the maintainer defines those terms and a submission workflow.

## Useful design feedback

Describe the problem, the affected workflow, the proposed change, and a testable acceptance criterion. Keep proposed features explicitly labeled. Include failure paths and permission boundaries, not just the ideal outcome.

## Requirements for future contributions

Submit only material you have the right to contribute. Identify third-party code, assets, datasets, and their licenses; preserve required attribution. Do not include confidential material, credentials, personal data, or content taken from projects without permission. Follow [PROVENANCE.md](PROVENANCE.md).

Disclose material AI assistance and review generated content yourself. AI generation does not establish that content is original, secure, or licensed for use. No contributor agreement or rights transfer is implied by this document.

## Review checklist

- Scope and user value are clear.
- Planned and verified behavior are distinguished.
- Tests cover failure and authorization boundaries.
- Evidence supports new factual claims.
- Dependency and asset provenance is recorded.
- No secrets or unnecessary personal information are included.

Security reports must follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), rather than a public discussion containing exploit details or sensitive data.
