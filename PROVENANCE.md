# Provenance policy

## Scope

This policy covers code, documentation, visual assets, dependencies, fixtures, and datasets proposed for LoopVero. It defines the intended intake process; it is not a legal opinion or a certification of independent development.

## Intake rules

1. Record where each imported component came from, its version or revision, its rights holder where known, and the applicable license or written permission.
2. Confirm that the intended use and distribution are allowed before including it.
3. Preserve notices, attribution, and license text where required. Do not remove origin information to change how an item appears to have been created.
4. Keep material with unknown or incompatible rights outside the distribution until resolved.
5. Do not import confidential material, secrets, or restricted datasets.
6. Record material AI assistance and review generated content for accuracy, security, and suspicious overlap; generation alone is not rights clearance.

## Initial package record

The initial prose, SVG geometry, and brand tokens were newly authored for LoopVero with AI assistance. No third-party source code, bundled fonts, stock artwork, external images, or runtime dependencies are included. The SVG uses basic geometric shapes; the identity has not undergone trademark or similarity clearance. These statements describe this initial package, not future contributions or a formal clean-room process.

## Record template

For future imported items, record: path or component; source URL or permission reference; revision; author or rights holder if known; license; required notices; modifications; reviewer; and distribution decision. Store private permission evidence privately and publish only the necessary notices.

## Before distribution

Review the actual release contents, not just this policy. Verify notices and licenses, remove private information, and reconcile all imported components against the intake record. Distribution remains gated on the project licensing decision.
